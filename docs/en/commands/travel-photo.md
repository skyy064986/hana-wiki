# Japan travel photos

## `/travelphoto <prefecture>`

Choose a Japanese prefecture in English and Hana will fetch a random travel reference photo from Pexels.

```text
/travelphoto prefecture: Hokkaido
```

## Important notes

- Images are **Pexels reference photos**, not photos taken by Hana.
- Hana makes a new search each time and intentionally does not cache photos.
- If Pexels finds nothing, try the prefecture again or choose another prefecture.
- Available in servers and through DM/User Install.

## Photos during AI chat

When Hana has a current-status destination or a verified trip record and you ask for a photo, it will try to send a reference image. Hana will not claim to have sent a photo until an attachment actually succeeded, and never substitutes an image from another prefecture.
